# Desk Watch 0.1.0-beta

First public test version. Windows 10/11 only. **Beta: still being tested.**

## What it does
- Watches your webcam while you study and rings an alarm for a phone in your hand or at your ear, leaving your desk,
  looking away, or falling asleep (eyes closed 6 s, head on the desk 15 s).
- Study sessions, a full-screen Focus mode (Pomodoro, Countdown, Count-up), a to-do list, a board, weekly routines and a
  day view, honest reports and history.
- Blocker mode: blocks sites (Chrome and Edge, with the included extension) and apps during a session, with a warning
  first, then the alarm.
- English and বাংলা. A dark and a light theme.
- Everything stays on your computer: no account, no cloud, no tracking; camera images are never saved.

## Install
Download `Desk-Watch-Setup-0.1.0-beta.exe` below. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns once:
click **More info**, check the name is Desk Watch, then **Run anyway**. Details in the README.

## Known limits
- The phone model was trained on one person, one room, one phone. It may miss your phone or see one that isn't there.
- All test clips were recorded at night, under one light, with glasses on. Glasses can hide closed eyes.
- A phone lying flat on the desk while you look down at it can be logged as sleepy.
- Firefox is not supported for site blocking.
